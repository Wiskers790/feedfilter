Keep the feedfilter folder where it is. Chrome runs the extension from that folder, so if it's in Downloads, move it somewhere permanent (like Documents) first, then load it again from there.

Your rulesClick the Feed Filter icon in the toolbar to switch rules on or off, make them stricter or looser, or add your own.
It learns from you"Show" on a hidden post makes that rule a bit less strict; "Hide like this" on a visible post makes it catch more.
