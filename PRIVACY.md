# Feed Filter privacy policy

_Last updated: October 12, 2026_

Feed Filter does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data.

**What happens on your computer.** When you browse X (x.com), the extension reads the text of the posts on the page
and checks them against your rules with an AI model that runs inside your browser, on your own graphics card.
Post text, your rules, your settings and the list of recently hidden posts stay on your device (in the browser's
extension storage). Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

**Network requests.** The only thing the extension downloads is the AI model itself (about 450 MB, once), from the
project's public GitHub release page (github.com). That request contains no information about you or your browsing.

**No accounts, analytics, or tracking.** There is no sign-up, no telemetry, no advertising, and no third-party code
that phones home.

**Removing your data.** Uninstalling the extension deletes its settings and the cached model.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Wiskers790/feedfilter/issues
